Privacy Policy

Compose Experiments · Effective 24 September 2026

In short: Compose Experiments does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no network access.

This policy covers the Compose Experiments app for Android and iOS and its web version at kevindesai777.github.io/compose-experiments. The app is a showcase of user interface experiments built with Compose Multiplatform, developed by Kevin Desai. Its source code is public on GitHub.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for, collect or store any personal information. Specifically:

  • There is no sign-up, login or user account.
  • The app requests no device permissions, including internet access, location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage.
  • The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking software.
  • Everything the app shows, including images, is bundled inside the app and rendered on your device.

Sharing of information

Because the app collects no data, there is nothing to share with or sell to anyone.

Third-party services

The app itself uses no third-party services. The stores and hosts that deliver it may collect information under their own policies, independently of this app:

  • Google Play, when you install the Android app: Google Privacy Policy.
  • Apple's App Store, when you install the iOS app: Apple Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the web version and this page, and may log technical information such as IP addresses: GitHub Privacy Statement.

External links

The app has a "View source on GitHub" link. Tapping it opens GitHub in your browser, where GitHub's privacy statement applies.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling personal data, this policy will be updated before that release and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised by opening an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
